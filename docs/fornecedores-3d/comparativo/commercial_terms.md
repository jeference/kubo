Scope: non-endpoint facts for a business that resells physical prints of objects generated by end customers through the API. No API calls were made and no accounts were created. Legal flags are a reader's notes, not legal advice. No recommendation is made here.

## API plans and pricing

### Takeaway
Tripo sells API credits as flat pay-as-you-go at $0.01 per credit from $1, with no subscription and no expiry, billed separately from Tripo Studio. Meshy has no standalone API purchase: the API needs a Pro plan or higher, and credits cost about $0.0125 to $0.02 depending on plan. Meshy's extra-pack prices could not be verified without a login, and its documents conflict on whether purchased credits expire.

### Cited Findings

**Tripo**
- "1 credit = $0.01 USD"; checkout through Stripe; "100 credits = $1.00 USD; you can also enter any amount from $1"; preset amounts of $50, $100 and $250 — [Tripo API pricing](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/pricing)
- Credits do not expire: "Our credits do not expire and remain valid indefinitely" — [Tripo Developers FAQ](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en); "Credits never expire" — [Support FAQ](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/other/support-faq.html)
- Volume discounts exist but no rates are published: "customized pricing and volume discounts for enterprise customers with large-scale usage needs", contact business@tripo3d.ai — [Tripo Developers FAQ](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en). The legacy FAQ gives business@vastai3d.com for the same purpose — [Support FAQ](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/other/support-faq.html)
- API and web app are separate: "Our webapp and API operate under two independent billing systems. Credits are not shared between them, and pricing differs" — [Support FAQ](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/other/support-faq.html). A Studio subscription does not include the API: "No. Tripo API and Tripo Studio operate under separate billing systems and require separate purchases" — [Tripo Developers FAQ](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en)
- Studio plans, which do not apply to the API: Free 200 credits per month; Pro $20 per month for 3,000 credits; Max $90 per month for 25,000; Team $110 per month per seat, or $55 billed annually. Studio packs include $100 for 10,000 + 2,000 credits and $1,000 for 100,000 + 30,000. These are not API prices — [Tripo Studio pricing](https://www.tripo3d.ai/pricing)
- API credits are prepaid and non-refundable: "API Credits are prepaid and non-refundable once purchased, except where required by applicable law" (s. 6.4, only in the developer copy of the terms) — [Tripo developer terms](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/terms)
- Failed and cancelled tasks are not charged, because credits are frozen at creation and returned on failure or cancellation — [Tripo billing](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/billing)
- Free API credits are described only without numbers. "generating a first API key grants a separate credit pool", and a game-focused developer program grants a larger one-time pool — [Tripo blog](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/ai-3d-api-free-credits-testing)

**Meshy**
- The API needs a paid plan: "You will need to first create a Meshy account and be on the pro tier or above to use the API" — [Meshy API page](https://www.meshy.ai/api). Free accounts cannot create API keys and can only try Image to 3D in the Playground through a built-in key — [Developer Platform docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/developer-platform)
- USD plan prices, from the structured data on the pricing page: Pro $20 per month for 1,000 credits; Premium $40 for 3,000; Ultra $100 for 8,000; Studio $70 for 5,500 shared credits; Pro yearly $240; Studio yearly $840 — [Meshy pricing](https://www.meshy.ai/pricing). The same Pro, Premium and Ultra figures appear in the [plan comparison](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12062933-which-meshy-plan-is-right-for-you-free-vs-pro-vs-premium-vs-ultra)
- Studio packages: 5,500 credits for $70, 16,800 for $210, 28,000 for $350 per month; extra seats are $10 per month — [Studio plan](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/14317306-understanding-the-meshy-studio-plan)
- The pricing page as fetched rendered prices in BRL: Pro R$107.90, Premium R$216, Studio R$380, plus a Starter plan at R$54 with no API access. It had no Ultra column — [Meshy pricing](https://www.meshy.ai/pricing). Starter is "exclusively available to users in some developing countries", at $10 per month for 250 credits — [Starter plan](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12927561-what-is-the-starter-plan-available-in-some-developing-countries)
- New subscribers get 50% off the first month on Pro, Premium and Ultra — [plan comparison](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12062933-which-meshy-plan-is-right-for-you-free-vs-pro-vs-premium-vs-ultra)
- API credits share the plan balance. The docs say "buy API credits in your subscription settings" — [Meshy API pricing](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/pricing); Playground requests use "your plan credits" — [Developer Platform docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/developer-platform); plugins and integrations "draw from the same credit balance at the same rates" — [API credit costs](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/16815622-how-many-credits-does-each-meshy-api-task-cost)
- Monthly plan credits do not roll over: "Features do not roll over from one month to the next, and once expired, unused Features have no value" (s. 2.10) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)
- Purchased credit expiry conflicts. The Terms say "Additional Credits remain available in your account for up to one (1) year from the date of purchase, after which any unused balance will automatically expire" (s. 2.10) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use). The pricing FAQ says "permanent credits do not expire" — [Meshy pricing](https://www.meshy.ai/pricing), and the docs say "Permanent and purchased credits don't expire" — [web app pricing docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/pricing)
- Volume pricing: "Enterprise volume pricing is available for committed usage" — [Meshy API page](https://www.meshy.ai/api). Enterprise is "an annual agreement, invoiced, with PO support" and priced by quote — [Meshy Enterprise](https://www.meshy.ai/enterprise)
- Free plan: 100 credits per month with no API access — [Free plan](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/15696428-what-is-included-on-the-free-plan); "the Free plan does not have API access, even if you purchase additional credits" — [API credit costs](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/16815622-how-many-credits-does-each-meshy-api-task-cost)
- Failed tasks are refunded automatically — [Task Lifecycle](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/task-lifecycle). Other fees are "non-cancelable and non-refundable" (s. 4) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)

### Inferences
- Meshy's per-credit figures only hold if the whole monthly allowance is used, since plan credits do not roll over. Low or uneven sales volume raises the effective Meshy price. Tripo has no such effect.
- Because Meshy's Terms contain an entire-agreement clause (s. 11.10), the Terms are the safer reading. Kubo should treat purchased credits as expiring after one year until Meshy confirms otherwise in writing.
- The Ultra plan did not appear in the pricing table as fetched, although structured data, help articles and the rate-limit docs all list it. If Ultra cannot be bought from Brazil, the $0.0125 rate needs Studio 3x or 5x ($210 or $350 per month), and the concurrency limit would be 60 rather than 100.

### Gaps
- Meshy extra credit pack prices: the pricing page lists "Purchase extra credits" and "20% OFF on Extra Credit Packs", but no pack price is visible without a login. So the marginal cost per credit above a plan's allowance is unverified.
- Meshy annual discount: structured data shows Pro yearly at $240, which equals 12 months at the monthly price. The annual toggle's rendered price could not be confirmed.
- Whether the BRL pricing and the Starter plan's availability are determined by location; no page says so for Brazil specifically.
- Enterprise rates and minimum commitments for both providers are not published.
- Tripo's free API credit amount has no official number. A figure of 2,000 appeared in a search summary, but I did not find it on any page I read, so it is not reported as fact.
- Minimum purchase at Meshy beyond the plan price is not stated.

## Rate limits, concurrency, latency, and SLA

### Takeaway
Tripo's defaults are the same for all accounts and differ by task category: 10 for H-series 3D, 5 for P-series, and only 1 for image generation. Increases are by request. Meshy's limits scale by plan, from 10 to 100 concurrent tasks in a single pool. Only Meshy publishes a median-latency table and a stated uptime figure, the latter for Enterprise; Tripo offers SLAs by negotiation.

### Cited Findings

**Tripo**
- Concurrency is per account and per category, each with an independent pool: H-series 3D 10; P-series 3D 5; image generation 1; animation 10; model processing (texture, convert, refine) 5; mesh operations 10 — [Tripo rate limits](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/rate-limits)
- Exceeding concurrency returns HTTP 429 with code 2000 and a `Retry-After` header. "To request higher concurrency limits, please contact our team via the support channel. Custom concurrency can be configured per category" — [Tripo rate limits](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/rate-limits)
- Request rate limits are per API key and vary by endpoint, with no numeric values published. They are exposed through `X-RateLimit-*` headers and return 429 with code 1007 — [Tripo rate limits](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/rate-limits). Image uploads are limited to 10 qps on the legacy docs — [legacy rate limits](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/get-started/rate-limits.html)
- Latency: H3.1 "~ 40s" without texture and "~ 120s" with texture — [H3.1 model page](https://developers.tripo3d.com/en/models/v3-1). The docs suggest a polling timeout such as 5 minutes — [Task Lifecycle](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/task-lifecycle)
- SLA: "Need custom volumes, dedicated support, or SLA guarantees? Contact our sales team" — [Tripo API pricing](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/pricing). The standard terms are "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE", with no warranty that use "will be uninterrupted" (s. 3.2) — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)
- Status page: [tripo3d.statuspage.io](https://tripo3d.statuspage.io/) has an "Openapi" component, and it showed "All Systems Operational" with no incidents on 2026-11-01
- Studio's limits of 1, 10, 100 and 200 concurrent tasks and its queue priority tiers belong to Studio plans — [Tripo Studio pricing](https://www.tripo3d.ai/pricing). The API docs I read describe no queue priority tiers — [Tripo rate limits](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/rate-limits)

**Meshy**

| Plan | Requests per second | Concurrent tasks | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | 20 | 10 | Default |
| Premium | 20 | 30 | Higher than Pro |
| Ultra | 20 | 100 | Highest |

Source: [Meshy rate limits](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/rate-limits)

- The limit counts PENDING and IN_PROGRESS tasks, and every task-creating endpoint checks it, including Text to Image, Remesh, Retexture and the printing endpoints. Every API request counts toward requests per second, including task polling. Over-limit responses are 429; the concurrency form has no `Retry-After` — [Meshy rate limits](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/rate-limits)
- Median times on production traffic in September 2026 ("typical times, not guaranteed") for Image to 3D — [Task Lifecycle](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/task-lifecycle):

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2K texture | 2 min | 3 min | 75 s | 60 s |
| 4K texture | 2.5 min | 3.5 min | not supported | 80 s |
| 8K texture | 3.5 min | 4 min | not supported | 2.5 min |

- PBR maps add 30 to 60 seconds and Ultra geometry adds up to 1 minute. Meshy advises waiting at least 60 minutes from `created_at` before giving up on a task — [Task Lifecycle](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/task-lifecycle)
- SLA: Enterprise is advertised with "Guaranteed highest rate limits, concurrency, and task priority — with 99.9% uptime SLA" — [Meshy API page](https://www.meshy.ai/api). Otherwise, "Meshy makes no guarantees regarding the quality, stability, uptime, or reliability of the Service" unless agreed in writing (s. 2.1) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)
- A status page exists at [status.meshy.ai](https://status.meshy.ai/), linked from the Meshy site footer

### Inferences
- Tripo's image generation limit of 1 is a likely bottleneck for Kubo's text/photo to 2D image step unless raised, or unless that step stays on another provider.
- At Meshy, 2D image tasks and 3D tasks compete for the same concurrency pool. At Tripo, they have separate pools. A burst of previews affects them differently.
- A Meshy Ultra plan offers 100 concurrent tasks at $100 per month, while Tripo's published default is 10 for H-series. Tripo's real ceiling depends on a request whose outcome is not published.

### Gaps
- Tripo's numeric requests-per-second limits are not published.
- Tripo's H-series image-to-3D latency is given only as an approximate model-level figure; no percentile data was found.
- Neither provider's actual uptime history could be read. Both status charts render client-side and Meshy's status API path returned 404.
- Tripo's API queue priority for enterprise customers is undocumented.

## Licensing and IP

### Takeaway
Both providers let paid users own and commercially exploit outputs, and neither requires attribution on paid plans. Tripo's help center explicitly approves 3D printing and print-on-demand. But both sets of terms have clauses that bear directly on Kubo's end-customer model: Tripo bars making the service available to end users without written consent, and Meshy bars reselling or redistributing the service or access to it and limits some access to internal use. Both place IP indemnity on the customer. Free tiers are unsuitable.

**Tripo (Terms last updated July 11, 2025)**

This is subject to compliance and to a license to Tripo "to use and display the Inputs and Outputs as may be necessary for Service provision on royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive basis" — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)
- Free users, s. 5.2.1 verbatim: "Tripo retains all rights, including without limitation the rights to use, copy, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, translate, create derivative works from, distribute, promote, transfer, authorize, license, optimize, derive revenue or other remuneration from, communicate to the public, perform, and display the Inputs and Outputs submitted or generated by Free Users" — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms). The Studio free tier is labelled "Public Models · Non-Commercial Use" — [Tripo Studio pricing](https://www.tripo3d.ai/pricing)
- **End-user clause, s. 3.2 verbatim:** "You shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: [...] make the Generative 3D Foundation Model Service available to any third party, including end users, without the prior written authorization and consent of Holymolly." — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)
- **Commercial-activity clause, s. 3.1 verbatim:** users shall not "(a) take any action or (b) make available any Inputs on or through Tripo that: [...] involves commercial activities and/or sales, such as contests, sweepstakes, barter, advertising, or pyramid schemes without Company's prior written consent" — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)

However, that you may use Outputs for lawful commercial or non-commercial purposes, so long as such Outputs are not distributed or made available to third parties in a manner inconsistent with this Agreement or applicable laws" — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)

## Content moderation and acceptable use

### Takeaway
Both APIs filter inputs automatically and surface rejections as errors, and neither publishes the filter's criteria. Meshy has a specific AUP that mentions third-party images, publicity rights, and minors; Tripo relies on general Terms provisions. Both make the customer responsible for rights in uploaded images, so trademarked characters and real-person likeness risk falls on Kubo.

### Cited Findings

**Tripo**
- Inputs must not infringe "any patent, trademark, trade secret, copyright, right of publicity or other right" or be "invasive of another's privacy" or "impersonate[] any person" (s. 3.1). Tripo "may, but is not obligated to, monitor or review Tripo and Inputs" and may remove them, terminate, or "disclose your identity or other information about you to any third party who claims that material posted by you violates their rights" — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)
- Minors: users must not "submit personal data of individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) without appropriate parental or guardian consent" (s. 3.2) — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms). Minimum user age is 13 (s. 1) — [Tripo Terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms)
- API behavior: HTTP 400, code 2008, `"The input is rejected due to violation of content policy"`, with the advice to "Modify the input content to avoid prohibited words or images" — [Tripo error handling](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/error-handling). "Your input may have triggered our content safety checks [...] If you believe your input is compliant, contact support" — [Support FAQ](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/other/support-faq.html)
- "Internal `banned` and `expired` outcomes are reported as `failed`. Check `error_code` and `error_message`" — [Task Lifecycle](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/task-lifecycle). Failed tasks are not charged — [Tripo billing](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/billing)
- Help guidance on inputs: "Fan art / existing IP characters: High risk. Do not use commercially without IP holder permission"; "Your own artwork / photos: Safe" — [Commercial use guide](https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/privacy-policy/how-to-use-tripo-models-commercially)

**Meshy**
- AUP s. 3 verbatim: "Using Meshy services to create, upload, or share content that violates third-party copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights is prohibited. This may include, but is not limited to: Entering text prompts designed to generate copyrighted, trademarked, or otherwise infringing content; Uploading an input or reference image that includes a third party's copyrighted content; Using a third party's personal information in violation of their privacy or data protection rights" — [Meshy AUP](https://www.meshy.ai/acceptable-use-policy)

- AUP s. 2 lists "Using unauthorized automated or scripting processes (such as bulk or automated uploading of content through a script)" as grounds for disabling accounts — [Meshy AUP](https://www.meshy.ai/acceptable-use-policy)
- Likeness: "If Your Community Content includes the name, image, likeness or voice of any identifiable person, You represent and warrant that You have obtained all necessary consents and releases from that person or their legal guardian" (s. 3.4). The sentence is written for Community Content; s. 3.4 more broadly warrants that User Content does not violate "privacy, or other rights" — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)
- Minors as users: minimum 14; under-18s need parental permission (s. 1.1) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use). "Our websites are NOT directed to children" under 14, and such data is deleted if discovered — [Meshy Privacy](https://www.meshy.ai/privacy-policy)

The filters check the text and the images together", with the advice to remove "suggestive or sensitive descriptions". `moderation_unavailable` (server_error): "Content moderation was temporarily unavailable, so Meshy failed the task instead of skipping the check" — [Meshy errors](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/errors). FAILED tasks are refunded — [Task Lifecycle](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/task-lifecycle)
- Meshy "reserve[s] the right to monitor your use", may delete User Content that violates the agreement (ss. 2.6, 3.2), and may suspend access (s. 5) — [Meshy Terms](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)
- Its printing addendum shows its own stance: Meshy may "reject or cancel any orders" to enforce restrictions, and customers warrant rights in submitted content — [Meshy Terms addendum](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)

### Inferences (flags for Kubo; not legal advice)
- Passing automated moderation is not a rights clearance. Neither filter is described as detecting trademarks, copyrighted characters, or real people, so Kubo needs its own upload attestation, screening, and takedown process, especially for photos of minors or celebrities.
- Because an account holder answers for all use, one customer's prohibited upload could put Kubo's single shared account at risk of suspension (Meshy s. 5; Tripo s. 3.1 and s. 10.3). Kubo's pre-screening also protects service continuity.
- Meshy's AUP item on "unauthorized automated or scripting processes" sits oddly next to an API product. It seems aimed at community uploads, but could be included in a confirmation request.
- Moderation failures need user-facing handling. Tripo's 2008 is a synchronous HTTP 400 in the docs, while internal bans surface as a failed task. Meshy's `moderation_unavailable` is transient and should be retried rather than shown as a content problem.

### Gaps
- Neither provider publishes moderation criteria, false-positive rates, or how photos of real people or children are treated by filters. Only testing can show this, and testing was out of scope.
- Tripo has no standalone acceptable use policy that I could find; a "Holymolly policies" reference in s. 3.2 points to nothing specific.
- Whether rejected inputs are retained, or used for review, is not stated by either.

## Payment, support, and enterprise contracts

### Takeaway
Tripo API purchases are by Stripe checkout, and Meshy self-serve is by card and wallets, with wire transfer and invoicing for Enterprise. Neither lists Pix or Boleto on the pages read. Enterprise channels at both are sales-led with negotiated limits, retention, and SLAs.

### Inferences
- Written answers to Kubo's core questions (end-customer use, photos, resale of prints, training, retention, DPA) are likely to come only through business@tripo3d.ai and Meshy sales or legal@meshy.ai. These conversations are effectively a prerequisite at both providers for this business model.
- Tripo's gross-up language and its 5% per day late fee are worth noting for Brazilian remittances, although prepaid credits should make late fees unlikely. Brazilian tax on service imports was not researched and applies whichever provider is chosen.
- Meshy's single-plan auto-renewal adds a commitment that Tripo's prepaid credits do not have.

### Gaps
- Whether Tripo's Stripe checkout or Meshy's checkout accept Brazilian cards, Pix, or Boleto was not verifiable without an account. Neither lists them.
- Enterprise minimums, pricing, and response times are not published for either.
- No published support response-time commitment exists for self-serve customers at either provider.
